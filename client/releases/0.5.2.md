# PimpMyElectron Client 0.5.2

- Slack Triage now respects muted conversations, including agent/app DMs and their threads. Muted items stay available in All and the matching Mentions, DMs, Channels, or Threads filters, but are excluded from Unread, unread highlighting, pill notifications and previews, and menu-bar/edge counts.
- Mute and unmute changes are observed from Slack’s existing local cache without additional API calls. Slack’s actual read state remains intact.
- A subtle 3-pixel inbox scroll indicator shows your position while scrolling, then fades after 1.2 seconds of inactivity. Its separate lane keeps it from covering conversation content.

Update from **Check for Updates…** in PME, or download the ZIP below. Stop active PME mod sessions before installing, then relaunch them to load the updated mods.

Apple Silicon, macOS 13.3 or later. Slack and Spotify are installed separately.
