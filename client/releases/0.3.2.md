Recover cleanly from shortcuts deleted or moved in Finder.

- Manage shortcuts now checks whether each saved launcher still exists and labels missing locations.
- Missing or unavailable shortcuts offer **Remove from list**, which clears the saved profile without needing the launcher app to exist or deleting files in Finder.
- Moving a shortcut does not automatically discard its profile. Launch it once from its new location to reconnect, or restore it to its saved location to clear the missing state.
- Remove also handles a shortcut deleted after the manager loaded its list. Existing shortcut identity checks still protect unrelated apps at the saved location.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 13.3+. Slack Triage and Spotify Menu Player remain included. Stop PME's helpers and quit the manager before replacing **PimpMyElectron.app** in Applications; quit Spotify normally when restarting its managed session. Existing settings and launch shortcuts remain compatible.
