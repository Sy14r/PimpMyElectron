# PimpMyElectron Client 0.5.1

- Drag the PME manager window using the strip across its top edge. Its controls and web content retain their normal interactions.
- Press **0** in Slack Triage to scroll to and highlight the first row in the current inbox list. This also works with a conversation open, without changing the conversation. Text fields and native overlays keep their normal keyboard behavior.

Update from **Check for Updates…** in PME, or download the ZIP below. Stop active PME mod sessions before installing.

Apple Silicon, macOS 13.3 or later. Slack and Spotify are installed separately.
