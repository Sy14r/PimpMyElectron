Smoother Spotify mini-player transitions and more patient shortcut startup.

- Opening or closing Library, Search, and Queue now smoothly resizes the player, shrinks the album art, and gently scales the track text without changing its line breaks.
- A single animation driver keeps the content and native popover in step. Artwork backgrounds retain their scale, and macOS Reduce Motion is respected.
- Spotify helper startup now waits up to 60 seconds for readiness, accommodating slower first-open checks without launching a duplicate helper. Timestamped launch logs distinguish macOS launch failures from readiness timeouts. The previously reported first-install failure has not been reproduced on a fresh Mac, so this also improves diagnosis if it recurs.

Requires Apple Silicon, macOS 13.3+, and the official Spotify or Slack app installed separately. Existing settings and launch shortcuts remain compatible. Stop PME's helpers and quit the manager before replacing **PimpMyElectron.app** in Applications; quit Spotify normally when restarting its managed session.
