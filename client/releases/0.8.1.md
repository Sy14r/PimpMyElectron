# PimpMyElectron 0.8.1

## Easier updates

- When active mod sessions block installation, PME now offers **Stop sessions and update** or **Not now**.
- Choosing Stop asks PME-managed Slack and Spotify instances to quit normally, stops their helpers, and waits for shutdown before continuing. No force-quitting is used. If an app cannot close, installation stays blocked with instructions to finish closing it and retry.
- **Not now** leaves your sessions running. Settings, sign-ins, and shortcut profiles are preserved. Relaunch your mods through PME or a shortcut after updating.
- Camera Pause-only sessions stop their helper without quitting ordinary Spotify. Mini Library sessions also close their managed Spotify instance, including when the widget has already been closed. Spotify playback may stop.
- Shortcut launches are blocked while the update is preparing, so a new session cannot interrupt installation.
- Fixed updater callbacks stalling while macOS waits for PME to quit.

## Install or update

Use **Check for Updates** in PME, or download the ZIP below. **When updating from 0.8.0 or earlier, you still need to stop mod sessions manually for this update.** Once 0.8.1 is installed, the new stop-and-update option is available for future updates.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 13.3 or later.
