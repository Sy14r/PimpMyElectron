Spotify joins PimpMyElectron with a menu-bar player and mini library.

- Hover or click the Spotify menu icon for now playing, play/pause, previous/next, shuffle, and repeat controls.
- Expand Library, Search, or Queue to browse liked songs, playlists, and albums, find music, play it, or add tracks to the queue.
- Choose Ambient wash, Artwork bleed, or Gallery glow. Artwork extends through the menu pointer and album tracks inherit their album artwork.
- Mini-player settings let you choose hover or click activation and a 0.1–5 second hover-dismiss delay. Pin keeps the compact player open when the pointer leaves.
- Create **Spotify — PME.app** beside Launch Spotify. Its bundled Spotify icon includes the orange PME plus badge; launch it from Finder, Spotlight, or the Dock without opening the manager.
- Spotify uses its existing sign-in, macOS Automation for basic playback, and a private inherited-pipe connection for the mini library, with no listening debugging port.

Requires Apple Silicon, macOS 13.3+, and the official Spotify app installed separately. Quit an ordinary Spotify session before the first PME launch, then allow Spotify control when macOS asks. Browsing/search can fetch data through Spotify; artwork comes from Spotify’s image CDN. Private client interfaces may need updates when Spotify changes. Keep PME installed for shortcuts to work.

Slack Triage remains included. Before replacing PME, close the manager and stop its helpers; quit Spotify normally when restarting its managed session. Existing settings remain on this Mac.
