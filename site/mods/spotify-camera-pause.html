SPOTIFY / CAMERA PAUSE

Camera on.
Music paused.

Let selected apps’ camera activity pause Spotify. When the camera session ends, resume only if Camera Pause was the one that paused it.

Set it up ↓Download PME ↗

Included with PME · Apple Silicon · macOS

01

Watch the apps you choose.

Pick installed conferencing apps or your browser in Camera Pause settings. Camera use is the signal, not simply having an app running.

02

Resume with a little breathing room.

Choose a delay from 0 to 30 seconds. A second watched app using the camera keeps playback paused; another camera session cancels the countdown.

03

Keep the final say.

Observed manual playback, track changes, and seeking cancel auto-resume. Use Keep Spotify paused to explicitly cancel a pending return to music.

GET STARTED

Make it part
of your day.

  1. 1
    Enable Camera Pause under Spotify.

    Use it by itself or together with Menu Player. On its own, it can attach to Spotify without restarting or injecting a library bridge.

  2. 2
    Select your camera apps.

    Open the camera menu-bar icon, or Camera Pause settings from the player gear. Choose apps and a resume delay.

  3. 3
    Test a camera session.

    Start music, turn your camera on in a watched app, then turn it off. Spotify should pause and resume after your configured delay.

GOOD TO KNOW

Local signals. No camera recording.

The mod reads macOS camera-activity diagnostics. It doesn’t open your camera, record audio or video, or inspect browser tabs. Spotify playback control requires Automation permission.

Per-app detection depends on a private macOS diagnostic format, verified on macOS 26.6.2. Other versions or restricted logging may make it unavailable. A browser preview counts as camera use, and all camera use in the selected browser counts.

A repeated Pause command while Spotify is already paused can’t be detected reliably. Use Keep Spotify paused when you don’t want it to resume.
