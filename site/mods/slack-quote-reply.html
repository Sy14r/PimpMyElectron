SLACK / QUOTE IN REPLY

A little context.
A clearer reply.

Quote a message into the conversation you’re already in. Keep the discussion moving without creating another thread.

Set it up ↓Download PME ↗

Included with PME · Apple Silicon · macOS

01

A quote, right in your draft.

Hover over a text message and choose Quote in reply. Its rendered text becomes a blockquote above what you’ve already written.

02

Keep your draft intact.

Existing text, formatting, and attachments stay in place. Review the result, keep typing, or use Slack’s Undo to remove the quote.

03

You decide when to send.

Nothing is sent automatically. In a DM or channel, it uses the main composer; inside a thread, it uses that thread’s existing composer.

GET STARTED

Make it part
of your day.

  1. 1
    Choose Quote in Reply under Slack.

    Enable it on its own or alongside Slack Triage. Stop and relaunch an existing managed session to change its mods.

  2. 2
    Open a conversation.

    Hover over a text message. The quotation-mark action appears just before More actions.

  3. 3
    Quote, edit, then send.

    Your composer receives the quote and focus. Review your reply and send using Slack as usual.

GOOD TO KNOW

Independent of the inbox.

Quote in Reply works in the standard Slack window and alongside Triage. It makes no API requests and stores no message history.

It quotes rendered text, line breaks, and readable mentions. Files, rich cards, and the source’s inline styling aren’t copied. Open a matching conversation before quoting from Search or Activity.
