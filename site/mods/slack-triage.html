03 / INBOX

One place to work through it.

DMs, channels, mentions, apps, and threads in an inbox at the edge of your screen. Filter down, open what matters, and keep your place.

JKMove through conversations
Technical guide ↗

A product walkthrough, not a connected Slack session. Inbox and pill screenshots use the current PME renderer with fictional content; hover and conversation panels are illustrations.

⌘

Keep your hands on the keyboard.

J/K to move, Enter to open, X to toggle read state. Search with ⌘K. Escape brings you back.

↳

Use the composer you know.

Slack’s native conversations, threads, and composer open alongside your inbox. Jump to full Slack whenever you need it.

◌

Make it feel like your desktop.

Choose a dock edge, inbox density, accent color, and translucency. Pick which workspaces can get your attention.

01

One inbox. Your workspaces.

Bring DMs, channels, apps, mentions, and threads together. Switch workspaces, filter the list, or give a thread a personal name.

02

Quiet means quiet.

Muted conversations stay out of Unread and pill notifications. Choose which workspaces can get your attention independently of your inbox filter.

03

As compact as you like.

Pick Expanded, Cozy, or Compact rows. Set your dock edge, accent color, and background translucency in Settings.

GET STARTED

Make it part
of your day.

  1. 1
    Enable Slack Triage in PME.

    Select Slack, choose Triage, then launch your managed session.

  2. 2
    Let Slack Home seed the inbox.

    PME opens Home first, then moves into the triage inbox. Choose one workspace or all of them.

  3. 3
    Use the edge or your keyboard.

    Hover to peek. Press ⌘⇧Y to toggle triage and ⌘⇧, for the keyboard cheat sheet.

GOOD TO KNOW

Still the official Slack app.

Slack owns sign-in, drafts, and sending. You can open the full Slack window from triage at any time.

The inbox observes existing Slack cache and traffic. It cannot promise a complete history or exact unread message totals. Opening native content can fetch messages and mark them read.
