SPOTIFY / MENU PLAYER

Your music.
One hover away.

A menu-bar home for what’s playing, your library, search, and the queue. Keep Spotify close without keeping its window open.

Set it up ↓Download PME ↗

Included with PME · Apple Silicon · macOS

01

Small player. Full control.

Play, pause, skip, shuffle, or repeat a collection or one track. Controls stay in sync with Spotify, even when its window is minimized.

02

Find the next thing.

Browse playlists, albums, and Liked songs. Search for songs and collections, start playback, or add a track to your queue.

03

Let the artwork set the mood.

Choose Ambient wash, Artwork bleed, or Gallery glow. Set hover or click to open, tune the dismissal delay, and pin the compact player while you browse.

GET STARTED

Make it part
of your day.

  1. 1
    Enable Menu Player in PME.

    Choose Spotify and Menu Player. Quit an ordinary Spotify session first, then launch through PME or a saved shortcut.

  2. 2
    Allow Spotify control.

    Accept the macOS Automation prompt. Your existing Spotify sign-in is reused; there’s no separate account to connect.

  3. 3
    Hover, listen, explore.

    Use the menu-bar icon for playback. Library, Search, and Queue expand the player; the gear opens player preferences.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your library, through Spotify.

Library and search use Spotify’s own client services on demand. These actions can make normal authenticated network requests; there is no background library polling.

Queue reordering, playlist editing, artist pages, local files, and saved shows aren’t supported yet. Pin keeps the compact player open on pointer exit; it isn’t an always-on-top setting.
